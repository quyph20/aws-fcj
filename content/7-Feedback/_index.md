### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at FCJ is exceptionally positive. The atmosphere is open, supportive, and highly conducive to productivity. Team members are approachable and always willing to provide assistance whenever challenges arise—even beyond regular working hours. The workspace is well-organized and comfortable, helping maintain focus and efficiency.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor’s guidance is thorough, patient, and insightful. Whenever I encounter difficulties, explanations are provided clearly and thoughtfully, ensuring I fully understand the concepts rather than just the solutions. I also appreciate the mentor’s approach of encouraging me to attempt problem-solving independently before stepping in with support.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks are highly aligned with my academic background, while still offering exposure to new knowledge areas that I had little experience with previously. This balance between familiarity and novelty has helped me reinforce foundational skills while expanding my technical and practical competencies in meaningful ways.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Throughout the internship, I had the opportunity to develop a wide range of essential skills—including project-management techniques, teamwork capabilities, and professional workplace communication. Additionally, the mentor frequently shared real-world insights and career-oriented advice, which provided valuable perspective for my long-term career development.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. When there are urgent projects, everyone works together and supports one another regardless of their position. This made me feel like a real part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible working hours when needed. In addition, having the opportunity to join internal training sessions is a big plus.
