# AI-Driven Development Workshop – Shaping the Future of Development  
**Venue:** AWS Event Hall, 26th Floor – Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Time:** 14:00 – 16:30, Friday, October 3, 2025  
**Speakers:** Mr. Toan Huynh, Ms. My Nguyen  
**Coordinators:** Diem My, Dai Truong, Dinh Nguyen  

---

## Event Objectives
- Share new trends in AI-orchestrated software development (AI-Driven Development).  
- Introduce AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AI-DLC) framework – a model integrating AI throughout the software development lifecycle.  
- Demonstrate two important tools: Amazon Q Developer and Kiro IDE Extension.  
- Analyze how AI enhances productivity, speed, and software product quality.  
- Guide the future role of AI in modern software development.  

---

##  Opening – Shaping the Future of Software Development
Mr. Toan Huynh opened with the theme “Shaping the Future of Development”, emphasizing the shift from traditional development models to AI-Orchestrated Development – where AI plays a supporting role, orchestrating processes from planning, design, programming to testing and deployment.

---

##  Challenges in Current Development Models
AI-Assisted Development and AI-Managed Development models still have limitations such as:
- Lack of stability  
- Hard to explain  
- Not truly achieving the promise of “AI acceleration”  

From there, the speaker introduced **AI-Driven Development (AI-DD)** – an approach balancing automation and human control.

---

##  AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AI-DLC) Model
AI-DLC consists of three evolutionary levels:

### **AI-Assisted Development**
AI supports:
- Code generation  
- Suggestions  
- Syntax checking  

### **AI-Driven Development**
AI participates in:
- Architecture design  
- Planning  
- Decision support  

### **AI-Managed Development**
AI automatically orchestrates the development process with human approval.

➡️ In this model, AI plays the role of **“intelligent coordinator”** while developers maintain decision authority.

---

##  Benefits of AI in Software Development
According to the “AI in Development – Outcomes” slide, AI brings 7 key benefits:

1. **Predictability** – Maintain progress & predict release timelines  
2. **Velocity** – Faster time-to-market  
3. **Quality** – Fewer errors, more stability  
4. **Innovation** – Inspire new ideas  
5. **Developer Engagement** – Improve motivation & efficiency  
6. **Customer Satisfaction** – Better user experience  
7. **Productivity** – Reduce time & increase output  

---

##  Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)
According to the “Where are developers spending time in the SDLC?” diagram, the development process includes stages: Explore & Plan → Create → Test & Secure → Review & Deploy → Maintain, Transform & Modernize.

AI helps shorten time in labor-intensive stages like testing, deployment and maintenance through automation and intelligent analysis.
---

##  AI-DLC Process Characteristics
Based on the slide “Key Workflow Features”, AI-DLC has:

- **Role Separation** – Clear responsibilities  
- **AI-Enhanced** – Each role has its own AI context  
- **Iterative** – Feedback loops across stages  
- **Template-Driven** – Unified templates: AIDLC  

---

##  AI in Each Development Stage
The slide “Each Stage” highlights:

- **Specific Context** for each role (PM, Architect, Developer)  
- **Clear Inputs/Outputs**  
- **Interactive Approach** – AI & human collaboration  
- **Documentation** – Continuous updates (prompts.md, dashboard.md…)  

---

##  Live Demo: Amazon Q Developer & Kiro IDE

### **Amazon Q Developer**
- AI assistant integrated in IDE  
- Auto-generates code, tests, docs, AWS architecture  
- Updates prompt.md & automates CI/CD  
- Demo: Create development plans, user stories, project management  
### **Kiro IDE**
- IDE extension for creating/organizing specification documents  
- AI generates:  
  - Feature descriptions  
  - API flows  
  - Backend code  
- Demo: Build Chat App with login + registration + token
