### Week 4 Achievements:

* WAF Pillar and Service Analysis: Selected a core service (e.g., EC2 or S3) and analyzed how that service meets the requirements of each pillar within the Well-Architected Framework

* AWS CloudTrail Configuration: Enabled and configured AWS CloudTrail to record administrative activities (API calls) within the account. Outcome: Able to track detailed "who, what, when, and where" information in the AWS environment.

* Governance Application Summary: Clearly understood that setting up auditing tools (like CloudTrail) and adhering to design frameworks (WAF) are essential steps for maintaining a stable, secure, and cost-optimized Cloud environment.
